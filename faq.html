Questions

Everything worth knowing before your first flight.

Do I need any flying experience?

None at all. Every flight is one-on-one with an instructor, and the 15-minute Basic session is built for your first time at the controls.

Which session should I choose?

For a fun first flight, pick an Entertainment session: Basic (15 min), Advanced (30 min, a full take-off to landing) or Premium (60 min, the complete experience). Deep learning sessions are structured training on the A320’s systems. Live ATC puts you in shared airspace with other pilots and live air traffic controllers. See all sessions and prices.

Can two people fly together?

Yes. The 60-minute two-pilot Deep learning session puts you both in the cockpit as a crew, working through full crew procedures together.

What is Live ATC?

In the Live ATC session you fly on VATSIM, an online network where other simulator pilots share the sky with you and live controllers give you instructions over the radio, just like a real flight.

How realistic is the simulator?

It’s a full 1:1 scale Airbus A320 flight deck, with every switch, panel and control where it sits on the real aircraft, and you can fly to more than 24,000 airports worldwide.
